# AuraShop Data Privacy & Monetization Policy

This privacy policy explains how AuraShop manages, processes, and monetizes customer data. We are committed to protecting your privacy while offering programs that allow you to participate in data monetization benefits.

---

## 1. Regional Data Protection & Sales Policies

Our treatment of customer data depends on your registered geographical billing location.

### 1.1 California Consumers (CCPA Compliance)
If your registered billing address is within the state of **California, United States**, your data is protected under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
*   **Data Sale Policy**: AuraShop **does not sell, rent, or trade** the personal information, browsing history, or purchase history of California residents to any third party under any circumstances.
*   **Opt-out**: By default, California residents are automatically opted out of all third-party data tracking pools.

### 1.2 Non-California Consumers (Global & Other Regions)
For customers registered in any state, territory, or country outside of California:
*   **Data Sale Policy**: AuraShop reserves the right to sell compiled customer data pools to external market research agencies.
*   **Anonymization Requirement**: Before any data is sold, it must go through our **Anonymization Pipeline**. All personally identifiable details (including full names, phone numbers, exact billing addresses, and raw email addresses) are permanently removed or hashed. The data is grouped into demographic aggregates (e.g., *"Males aged 25-34 in Texas purchased sneakers"*).

---

## 2. The AuraShop Partner Program

Customers can choose to opt into our premium **Partner Program** to monetize their shopping data.

### 2.1 Program Mechanics
By enrolling in the Partner Program, you grant AuraShop explicit consent to bypass the standard anonymization pipeline and sell detailed, individual-level shopping preferences directly to our advertising networks.
*   **Monetization Scope**: Data shared includes product clicks, shopping cart abandonment rates, category interest levels, and browsing session duration.

### 2.2 Payout Kickbacks (AuraCoins)
In exchange for your participation in the Partner Program, AuraShop pays you a direct financial kickback in the form of **AuraCoins** (our digital store currency).
*   **Earnings Ratio**: You earn 10 AuraCoins for every $100.00 spent on our platform, plus 2 AuraCoins per month of active sharing.
*   **Conversion Rate**: 1 AuraCoin is equivalent to $1.00 USD of store credit, redeemable at checkout.

### 2.3 Expiry Constraints
AuraCoins earned through the Partner Program are subject to strict expiration rules.
*   **Expiration Window**: All AuraCoins expire exactly **one year (12 calendar months)** from the date they are deposited into your account.
*   **Forfeiture**: Expired AuraCoins are permanently forfeited and cannot be recovered or refunded.

---

## 3. Account Deletion and Opt-Out

You can opt out of the Partner Program or request complete account deletion at any time via your account settings dashboard. 
*   **De-registration**: Upon opting out, data collection stops immediately, but any previously accumulated AuraCoins will remain in your account until their original expiration date.
