# AuraShop Return & Refund Policy

Welcome to the AuraShop customer returns center. We strive to provide a transparent and structured return policy tailored to your customer tier level. Please read this document carefully to determine your eligibility for an item return and refund.

---

## 1. Membership Tiers & Return Windows

Our return policy is governed by your active customer membership level (Bronze, Silver, or Gold) at the time of purchase.

### 1.1 Bronze Tier
The Bronze tier is our standard entry-level membership. 
*   **Return Window**: 7 calendar days from the date of delivery.
*   **Eligibility Conditions**: Returns are accepted **only** if the item arrives damaged, defective, or is not working (hardware/electronic failure). You must provide photographic proof of damage upon opening the return ticket.
*   **Exceptions**: Clothing, apparel, and footwear are strictly **excluded** from returns under the Bronze tier. If you purchase a shirt, jacket, or shoes, you cannot return them for a refund under this tier, even if the fit is incorrect.

### 1.2 Silver Tier
The Silver tier offers expanded protections for regular shoppers.
*   **Return Window**: 14 calendar days from the date of delivery.
*   **Eligibility Conditions**: Returns are accepted for all items that arrive damaged, defective, or non-functional.
*   **Scope**: Unlike the Bronze tier, **all product categories** (including clothing, apparel, and electronics) are eligible for returns under the Silver tier if they are defective. However, returns for change-of-mind or size exchange are still not permitted.

### 1.3 Gold Tier
The Gold tier is our premium membership, offering the highest level of shopper confidence.
*   **Return Window**: 30 calendar days from the date of delivery.
*   **Eligibility Conditions**: Customers can return items for **any reason**, including change of mind, incorrect size, or simply if the customer feels like it. 
*   **Requirements**: Items returned for change of mind must be in their original, unopened packaging, and in resaleable condition with all tags attached.

---

## 2. General Return Requirements

Regardless of your tier, all returns must satisfy the following baseline standards:
1.  **Proof of Purchase**: A valid AuraShop order ID (beginning with #) must be supplied.
2.  **Original Accessories**: All boxes, manuals, charging cables, and promotional items must be returned inside the package.
3.  **Return Shipping Labels**: Return shipping labels are provided free of charge for Gold tier members. Bronze and Silver tier members will have a standard shipping fee of $5.99 deducted from their final refund amount.

---

## 3. Refund Processing Timelines

Once our warehouse receives and inspects the returned item, we will process the refund to your original payment method. 
*   **Inspection**: Takes 3–5 business days.
*   **Credit Posting**: Bank processing times vary, but funds typically post to your credit card or bank account within 5–7 business days following inspection approval.
